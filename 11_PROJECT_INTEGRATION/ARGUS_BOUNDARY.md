# Argus Integration Boundary

Argus is a consumer of the reusable infrastructure.

## Infrastructure responsibilities

- model routing;
- agent runtime;
- multi-agent coordination;
- web/browser tool capability;
- permission/sandbox;
- context and memory;
- sessions/checkpoints;
- events/observability;
- resource lifecycle.

## Argus responsibilities

- research request interpretation;
- evidence taxonomy;
- evidence corpus;
- research strategy;
- source/entity/event knowledge model;
- research-specific evaluation;
- research synthesis;
- research-specific confidence/unknowns.

The infrastructure must not absorb Argus-specific evidence logic.
